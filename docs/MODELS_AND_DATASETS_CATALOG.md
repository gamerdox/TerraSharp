## 1. Why Were These Models Chosen and for What Purpose?

In life-safety disaster management and civil defense, **black-box opacity is an operational hazard**. When an emergency commander orders the evacuation of thousands of residents from a tea-estate hamlet, that decision cannot be justified by an uninterpretable neuron activation in a deep neural network. It requires an auditable, causal factor breakdown grounded in physical geotechnical principles.

### 1.1 Why We Rejected Black-Box Deep Learning (CNNs, LSTMs, Random Forests)
1. **Explainable Civil Defense Trust:** Response teams require explicit causal explanations: *"This village is in Critical Alert because the slope is 28° + soil saturation is 82% + 24h rainfall is 185 mm, exceeding the Caine failure threshold by 120%."*
2. **Hard Physical Boundary Enforcement:** Purely statistical ML models lack physical constraints and frequently produce absurd predictions—such as predicting catastrophic landslides on flat parking lots or during dry summer days if spurious correlations exist.
3. **Universal Global Generalizability:** Deep learning models overfit to specific training basins and suffer catastrophic domain shift when deployed in unmonitored mountain ranges. In contrast, physical laws (Mohr-Coulomb limit equilibrium, Horn finite differences, Darcy's drainage decay) are universally valid across any terrain on Earth.

## 4. Standardized Physical Metrics & Mathematical Derivations

| Metric Symbol | Metric Full Name | Physical Unit | Derivation Formula / Method | Input Range | Normalized Scale $[0, 1]$ | Physical Rationale & Critical Thresholds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **$\theta$ (theta)** | Terrain Slope Gradient | Degrees (°) | $\theta = \arctan\left(\sqrt{(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x})^2 + (\frac{\partial z}{\partial y})^2}\right) \times \frac{180}{\pi}$ via Horn (1981) | $0.0^\circ – 90.0^\circ$ | $S_{\text{slope}} = \min\left(1.0, \max\left(0.0, \frac{\theta - 15.0}{30.0}\right)\right)$ | Primary driver of downslope gravitational shear stress. Slopes $< 15^\circ$ are geotechnical false-alarm zones; slopes $> 45^\circ$ saturate failure potential. |
| **$\psi$ (psi)** | Terrain Slope Aspect | Azimuth Degrees (°) | $\psi = \left(\text{atan2}(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}, -\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}) \times \frac{180}{\pi} + 360^\circ\right) \pmod{360^\circ}$ | $0.0^\circ – 360.0^\circ$ | Un-normalized (Direct physical azimuth) | Determines windward vs leeward exposure to monsoon storm fronts and solar-driven soil moisture drying. |
| **$A$** | Contributing Catchment Area | Number of Cells / km² | Topological sort of steepest D8 flow paths: $A(r, c) = 1 + \sum A(\text{upstream neighbors})$ | $1 – 2,500\text{ cells}$ | $S_{\text{flow}} = \frac{\log_{10}(A) - \log_{10}(A_{\min})}{\log_{10}(A_{\max}) - \log_{10}(A_{\min})}$ | Identifies stream channels, ravines, and drainage bottlenecks where torrential runoff concentrates into flash floods and debris torrents. |
| **$R_{24\text{h}}$** | 24-Hour Storm Precipitation | Millimeters (mm) | $R_{24\text{h}} = \sum_{t=0}^{23} R(t)$ | $0.0 – 500.0+\text{ mm}$ | $R_{24\text{h, norm}} = \text{clip}\left(\frac{R_{24\text{h}}}{250.0}, 0, 1\right)$ | Represents the immediate short-term meteorological trigger. Monsoon cloudbursts exceeding $200\text{ mm}/24\text{h}$ initiate widespread shallow translational slides. |
| **$R_{3\text{d}}$** | 3-Day Cumulative Rainfall | Millimeters (mm) | $R_{3\text{d}} = \sum_{t=0}^{71} R(t)$ | $0.0 – 800.0+\text{ mm}$ | $R_{3\text{d, norm}} = \text{clip}\left(\frac{R_{3\text{d}}}{450.0}, 0, 1\right)$ | Captures prolonged storm systems that saturate topsoil horizons and exhaust infiltration capacity. |
| **$R_{15\text{d}}$** | 15-Day Antecedent Build-up | Millimeters (mm) | $R_{15\text{d}} = \sum_{t=0}^{359} R(t)$ | $0.0 – 1,500.0+\text{ mm}$ | $R_{15\text{d, norm}} = \text{clip}\left(\frac{R_{15\text{d}}}{1000.0}, 0, 1\right)$ | Long-term moisture reservoir that raises regional water tables and weakens saprolite shear strength before the final storm trigger. |
| **$I_0$** | Current Hourly Intensity | mm / hour | $I_0 = \frac{R_{24\text{h}}}{24.0}$ | $0.0 – 80.0+\text{ mm/hr}$ | Direct physical rate | Compared directly against Caine (1980) $I_{\text{crit}} = 4.29\text{ mm/hr}$. Exceeding $I_{\text{crit}}$ triggers the `THRESHOLD_BREACHED` state. |
| **$\alpha$ (alpha)** | Rainfall Intensification Rate | $\text{mm} / \text{hour}^2$ | $\alpha = \frac{dI}{dt} \approx \frac{I(t) - I(t-3)}{3.0}$ | $-5.0 – +15.0\text{ mm/hr}^2$ | Direct acceleration trend | Drives the dynamic lead-time estimator. Positive $\alpha$ indicates storm intensifying toward failure; negative $\alpha$ indicates clearing weather. |
| **$\text{AMI}$** | Antecedent Moisture Index | Millimeters (mm) | $\text{AMI} = \sum_{k=0}^{14} R_k \cdot (0.85)^k$ | $0.0 – 350.0+\text{ mm}$ | $S_{\text{soil}} = \min\left(1.0, \max\left(0.0, \frac{\text{AMI}}{250.0}\right)\right)$ | Physical proxy for soil profile saturation. Models exponential gravity drainage loss (15% per day) without requiring buried physical sensors. |
| **$\text{Risk}_{\text{ls}}$** | Weighted Landslide Risk Index | Dimensionless $[0, 1]$ | $0.35 \cdot S_{\text{rain}} + 0.30 \cdot S_{\text{slope}} + 0.20 \cdot S_{\text{soil}} + 0.15 \cdot S_{\text{hist}}$ | $0.00 – 1.00$ | Continuous index | Overall catastrophic failure probability index. Drives village-level emergency alert state transitions. |
| **$\text{Risk}_{\text{ff}}$** | Flash Flood Concentration Index | Dimensionless $[0, 1]$ | $\min\left(1.0, 0.50 \cdot S_{\text{rain}} + 0.30 \cdot (1 - S_{\text{slope}}) + 0.20 \cdot S_{\text{soil}}\right)$ | $0.00 – 1.00$ | Continuous index | Inundation risk index highlighting valley bottoms, stream beds, and settlement floodplains. |
| **$T_{\text{lead}}$** | Estimated Warning Lead Time | Hours | $T_{\text{lead}} = \max\left(1.0, \min\left(72.0, \frac{I_{\text{crit}} - I_0}{\alpha}\right)\right)$ | $0.0 – 72.0\text{ hours}$ | Discrete status: Breached (0h), Imminent (1–72h), Safe Margin (48h+) | Actionable operational evacuation window provided to emergency civil defense commanders. |
