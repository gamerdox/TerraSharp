# Scientific Limitations & Boundary Conditions

To maintain the highest standards of scientific integrity, the **SH-304 Early Warning System** explicitly declares all physical boundaries, data limitations, model assumptions, and operational disclaimers.

---

## 1. Early Warning vs. Deterministic Prediction

> [!WARNING]
> **Data-Fusion Early Warning vs Guaranteed Prediction:**  
> This system is an **early-warning data fusion heuristic**. A high or critical risk score indicates favorable environmental conditions for slope instability or flash flooding (heavy precipitation, steep terrain, high antecedent moisture, historical susceptibility). It is **NOT** a guarantee that a mass movement or flood surge will occur at an exact minute or coordinate.

---

## 2. Soil-Saturation Proxy Disclosure

- **Proxy Nature:** In the base MVP, soil saturation is calculated via an **Antecedent Moisture Index (AMI)** proxy derived from 3-day and 15-day GPM IMERG rainfall accumulation with exponential drainage decay.
- **Limitation:** It is **NOT** a direct in-situ soil moisture sensor reading, piezometric pore-water pressure measurement, or microwave radiometer observation (e.g. SMAP / Sentinel-1 SAR). Soil depth, infiltration capacity, lithology, and geotechnical soil cohesion vary micro-locally and are not directly resolved.
- **System Labeling:** All soil layers are strictly tagged with `ProvenanceTag.PROXY`.

---

## 3. Lead-Time Threshold (Caine 1980 Reference)

- **Reference Model:** Lead-time is evaluated using the empirical global rainfall intensity-duration equation published by Caine (1980):
  $$I = 14.82 \times D^{-0.39}$$
- **Non-Guarantee:** The system projects estimated time to threshold breach based on current rainfall intensification rate $\frac{dI}{dt}$. It does **NOT** state "a landslide will occur in $X$ hours".
- **Local Calibration Caveat:** Caine (1980) is a global empirical benchmark compiled from worldwide events. It is not locally calibrated to the Western Ghats (lateritic soils) or the Himalayas (fractured crystalline/sedimentary rocks). In production, this should be superseded by localized thresholds from the Geological Survey of India (GSI) or IMD.
- **System Presentation:** The system explicitly accompanies all lead times with a $\pm 30\%$ uncertainty interval and a disclaimer.

---

## 4. Flash-Flood Modeling Boundary

- **Topographic Concentration vs Hydrodynamics:** The flash-flood risk layer combines 24h precipitation volume, D8 upstream flow accumulation, and valley bottom flatness.
- **No 2D Hydrodynamic Routing:** The MVP does **NOT** run full 2D Saint-Venant hydraulic wave routing, Manning roughness friction, or dam breach inundation models. It identifies topographic water pooling funnels.

---

## 5. NASA Global Landslide Catalog (GLC) Incompleteness

- **Reporting Bias:** The NASA GLC compiles reported landslide events from media, government reports, and humanitarian agencies. Consequently:
  - Heavily populated corridors and major highways have high report density.
  - Remote, forested, or uninhabited mountainous terrain has severe under-reporting.
- **Mitigation:** Unrecorded cells are imputed using the AOI-median baseline rather than zero to avoid treating lack of records as absence of hazard.

---

## 6. Simulated Evacuation Alerts

- **Operational Scope:** All evacuation alerts and relief center dispatches generated by the alert engine are **SIMULATED** decision-support advisories.
- **No Live SMS/Broadcast:** The system does not dispatch real SMS, cell-broadcast, or WhatsApp notifications to citizens. Official evacuations must be ordered exclusively by the District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA) and State Disaster Management Authority (SDMA).
